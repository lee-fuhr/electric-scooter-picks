Segway vs Ninebot: are they the same brand?

Updated June 2026Independently researchedNo paid placement.

Quick answer

Ninebot is the parent company that acquired Segway in 2015; today 'Ninebot' and 'Segway' are distinct product lines within the same company. In practice, 'Segway' is used for premium, higher-range models (like the Max series), while 'Ninebot' covers entry-level and mid-range scooters (E and F series). For most buyers, the name on the box indicates the feature tier rather than any corporate difference.

The corporate history: how ninebot acquired segway

In 2015, the Chinese company Ninebot acquired Segway, the American pioneer of personal electric transport. This merger brought together Segway’s brand recognition and patent portfolio with Ninebot’s manufacturing scale and supply chain efficiency. Today, both brands operate under the same parent company, Ninebot Inc., but they target different market segments. While the acquisition was a major business move, from a consumer perspective it means that every new scooter from either brand shares the same engineering DNA and quality control standards. The distinction is now purely about positioning, Segway stands for the company’s highest-spec offerings, while Ninebot covers the more affordable and mid-range models.

How the naming convention actually works today

After the acquisition, Ninebot remained the corporate parent, but the brand names have been reorganized. “Segway” is now used as a premium sub-brand for the company’s most capable electric scooters. The “Ninebot” name appears on entry-level and mid-range models. To confuse matters, some scooters carry both names, for example, the “Segway Ninebot Max”. to signal that it is a Segway-tier product built by Ninebot. On the box, you’ll often see the Ninebot logo alongside Segway, but the key is the model series: E-series, F-series, and Max series. The dual branding is a marketing choice, not a sign of a joint venture. The name that comes first (usually Segway for high-end models) tells you the intended market position.

Product tiers: entry ninebot e-series vs mid-range f-series vs premium segway max

The lineup is neatly tiered. The Ninebot E-series (models like the E22 or E25) is the entry point, compact, lightweight, with solid or small pneumatic tires, and a range suited for short trips under a few miles. The Ninebot F-series (e.g., F30 or F40) steps up to larger tires, a slightly more powerful motor, and a longer range, making it a solid choice for everyday commuting without breaking the bank. The Segway Max line (such as the G30 or the newer models) represents the premium tier. These scooters have larger decks for comfort, wider pneumatic tires (usually 10 inches), a more powerful motor with higher torque, and a significantly greater real-world range, often double that of the F-series. The Max also typically includes better suspension, a more robust frame, and integrated front and rear lights. The price increases accordingly, but so does the durability and daily usability.

Practical differences between a mid-range ninebot and a premium segway model

If you compare an F-series Ninebot to a Segway Max, the differences are tangible. The Max has a larger deck, longer and wider, which makes longer rides more comfortable. Its 10-inch pneumatic tires absorb bumps much better than the 9-inch or solid tires on the F-series, and it usually includes a front suspension fork. The motor on the Max is rated for more continuous power, so hill climbing and acceleration feel stronger. Range is the biggest differentiator. A mid-range Ninebot might cover 10 to 15 miles in real-world conditions, while a Segway Max can go 20 miles or more on a charge, depending on rider weight and terrain. The build quality on the Max also feels sturdier, with thicker tubing and a reinforced stem latch. However, the Max is heavier and more cumbersome to carry, while the F-series strikes a better balance of weight and portability.

When should the name on the box matter to you?

For budget-conscious buyers who only need a scooter for short errands or last-mile trips, the Ninebot-branded E-series or F-series offer excellent value. You get well-built scooters with reliable braking and battery management, at a much lower cost than the Segway Max. The name doesn’t matter, the performance fits the price. For commuters who depend on their scooter for daily travel of several miles, the Segway Max line justifies its premium. The longer range, better ride quality, and more durable construction reduce the risk of breakdowns and flat tires. If you can afford the extra weight and price, the Segway name on the box translates to a noticeably better commuting experience. In short, choose Segway when you need maximum reliability and range; choose Ninebot when you want the most scooter for the money.

How to decode the model number without getting confused

Ninebot and Segway model numbers follow a pattern that reveals the tier. For Ninebot-branded scooters, the letter indicates the series: E for entry, F for mid-range. The number often hints at the battery capacity or range (for example, an F40 typically has a larger battery than an F30). For Segway-branded models, the Max line uses “G30” or similar designations, the G stands for generation, and higher numbers indicate newer or more specced versions. Don’t get thrown by dual branding: a scooter labeled “Segway Ninebot Max G30” is a Segway Max model, not a mid-range Ninebot. If you see “Ninebot KickScooter” with no Segway mention, it’s almost certainly an E- or F-series. When in doubt, look for the series letter (E, F, G) on the side of the deck or in the product description. That letter, more than the brand name, tells you what tier you’re getting.

Frequently asked questions

Are Segway and Ninebot scooters made by the same company?

Yes, Ninebot is the parent company that owns the Segway brand. All current electric scooters from both names are designed and manufactured by the same entity, under the same quality standards.

Which is better, a Ninebot F-series or a Segway Max?

The Segway Max is better for range, ride comfort, and long-term durability. The Ninebot F-series is better if you value lower cost, lighter weight, and easier portability for short trips.

Why do some scooters say ‘Segway Ninebot Max’ instead of just one name?

That dual branding marks the scooter as a Segway-tier product (premium) that is built by Ninebot. It’s a way to signal the high-end lineage while acknowledging the parent company. Other models like the Ninebot E22 omit the Segway name entirely.

Does a Ninebot scooter have the same reliability as a Segway?

Generally, yes, both share the same engineering team and manufacturing quality. The difference is in features and components: the Segway Max uses larger tires, stronger motors, and bigger batteries, which can improve long-term reliability in heavy use, but the Ninebot E and F series are still very dependable for their intended use.

Can I use Ninebot app features with a Segway scooter?

Yes, all current Segway and Ninebot scooters are compatible with the same Ninebot app, which allows you to adjust settings, lock the scooter, and view ride data. The app experience is identical regardless of the brand on the stem.

If I see a model number like ‘G30’ or ‘E22’, what does the letter tell me?

The letter indicates the product series: ‘E’ for entry-level, ‘F’ for mid-range, and ‘G’ for the premium Segway Max generation. The number usually tracks battery size or iteration, higher numbers generally mean larger batteries or newer releases.

In shortWhen shopping for a scooter from these two brands, don’t get hung up on the name, look at the model series letter. If you need a sturdy, long-range commuter and can handle the extra weight, the Segway Max line is worth the premium. If you want a reliable, lightweight scooter for shorter trips or a tighter budget, a Ninebot E or F series will serve you well. Both names deliver solid performance; the tier is what matters.
